===============================
        RACER
===============================
LEFT / RIGHT arrows - move car
R - restart after game over
Q - quit after game over

===============================
        SNAKE
===============================
UP arrow    - move up
DOWN arrow  - move down
LEFT arrow  - move left
RIGHT arrow - move right
R - restart after game over
Q - quit after game over

===============================
        PAINT
===============================
Left mouse button - draw / select tool / select color
Pencil  - freehand drawing
Rect    - click and drag to draw rectangle
Circle  - click and drag to draw ellipse
Eraser  - freehand erasing
+/-     - change brush size
C       - clear canvas
